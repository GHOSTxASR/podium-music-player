# Industrial direction — references (2026-10-03)

Principles only; nothing copied. Implemented in D-29.

| Reference | What we learned | What Podium adapts | What Podium does differently |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teenage Engineering OP-1 / TP-7 ([design write-up](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/teenage-engineering), [inside design at TE](https://read.cv/jdsimcoe/inside-design-at-teenage-engineering)) | Matte hardware as the personality; each mode owns the whole screen with no permanent chrome; physical controls map 1:1 to what's on screen; constraints celebrated, not apologised for; monospace type. | Matte black body and wheel, flat surfaces, no gloss; a knurled wheel band that turns in detent steps; the display is a quiet instrument UI; selection is an indicator, not a highlight. | Keeps Podium's grotesk (Instrument Sans) for legibility on a small display instead of monospace. |
| iPod classic 6th gen split menu ([iPod Wiki](https://ipodwiki.com/wiki/IPod_classic_(6th_Generation))) | Upper-level menus split: the list on one side, art for the selected section on the other. | The two-zone language: the list plus a glimpse of what the selection leads to. | The glimpse is the next column of one continuous sheet (the "paper"), with the previous column peeking in on the other side; it applies to every list, not only the main menu. |
| Cover Flow ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cover_Flow), [history](https://512pixels.net/2023/10/the-history-of-cover-flow/)) | Centre cover face-on; neighbours angled like a record rack, on black. | Restrained perspective (58°), neighbours smaller and dimmer with distance, wheel-driven, Center opens the album. | No reflections or gloss — the covers carry all the colour; the UI around them stays matte. |
| User-provided Layers iPod-redesign animation ([layers.to](https://layers.to/layers/clyhce7470008ih0cmotj33cv--ipod-redesign-animation)) | (Page renders client-side; principles taken from the brief's description.) Horizontal, spatial transitions: forward moves the content left, back returns it. | The paper model: every screen is a column; forward slides left (header title included), back slides right; the leaving screen's glimpses yield to the arriving column instead of flying across. | — |
