# Now Playing — visual references (2026-10-03)

Principles only; no assets were copied. Implemented in D-28.

| Reference | What we learned | What Podium adapts | What Podium does differently |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple, *iPod classic User Guide* (160 GB, ch. 3 "Listening to Music") — [PDF](https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/6GJYWVAV/user/ma1195_ipod_classic_160gb_user_guide.pdf) | Now Playing shows album art, title, artist, album, "N of M", elapsed/remaining. The wheel sets volume; **Center cycles** scrubber → Genius → shuffle → rating → lyrics; press-and-hold Center opens more commands; hold Play/Pause switches the device off. | The wheel contract: volume by default, Center cycles volume → scrubber → action row; hold Center = More; hold ⏯ = off. "N of M", album line, art + text hierarchy; art-left composition on landscape screens. | Actions are a small row (shuffle, repeat, favorite, Up Next, More) instead of sliders; rating becomes a favorite toggle. |
| Apple, iPod classic tech specs — [support.apple.com/112601](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/112601) | 2.5-inch, 320 × 240 (4:3) LCD set into the face above the wheel. | A display with a fixed physical proportion (portrait 0.60–0.82, landscape 1.20–1.70), recessed in a bezel, never full-bleed. | Portrait on phones, and as large as the wheel allows. |
| Apple Music Now Playing (iOS 26 coverage: [What Hi-Fi](https://www.whathifi.com/smartphones-tablets/apple-announces-ios-26-with-a-new-liquid-glass-design-and-an-upgraded-apple-music-app), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/?p=1005747)) and the play/pause artwork scale ([design guide](https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/apple-music)) | Artwork is the centrepiece; it "breathes" (≈ 0.92 scale paused, full when playing); a backdrop tinted from the art; transport bare and large, secondary actions quieter. | Breathing artwork (0.93 / 1.0, spring); art-derived blurred environment with a brightness-keyed scrim; bare primary transport; quieter secondary row. | Everything stays inside the virtual screen; no full-screen takeover; the wheel, not on-screen buttons, is the primary input. |
| Apple Music Classical ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2023/03/27/apple-music-classical-design-looks/), [Michael Tsai](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2023/03/29/apple-music-classical/)) | No blurred background; all metadata visible next to the art and transport. | Strict text hierarchy (title > artist > album) and a fixed status slot so metadata never pushes the layout. | Keeps a restrained artwork environment. |
| Apple, *Adopting Liquid Glass* — [developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/adopting-liquid-glass.md) and HIG Materials | Glass is a functional layer above content; use it sparingly on custom controls; group related actions in one glass container; don't stack glass; content (art) stays content. | One glass capsule for the secondary actions; the artwork is never glass and never framed by glass; the focus lens sits beneath the focused action. | Glass also marks the device's physical controls (wheel, power) on the Glass finish. |
| Album-art transitions in players ([Symfonium forum](https://support.symfonium.app/t/album-artwork-transitions/10365)) | Users notice abrupt swaps; same-art tracks shouldn't animate. | Direction-aware crossfade with slight scale and travel; no transition when the next song shares the cover; crossfade only with reduced motion. | No 3-D flips or page curls. |
