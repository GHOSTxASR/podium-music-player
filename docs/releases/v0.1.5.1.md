# Podium 0.1.5.1

Released 2026-11-03. Tag `v0.1.5.1`. A small update over 0.1.5, not a version of its own: the full
list of changes is in [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md); the decisions behind them are D-109 and
D-110 in [decision-log.md](../decision-log.md).

**Podium 0.1.5.1** adds a nostalgic Now Playing with no buttons on it, keeps a song from going into
a playlist twice, and lets Now Playing's screen relax when the tray slides away.

### What's new
- **A nostalgic Now Playing:** Settings ▸ Now Playing switches between New and Nostalgic. The
  nostalgic screen is the one Podium had before the redesign with the playback buttons taken out —
  the cover beside its facts, the song centred under it, the seek bar — and the vinyl's dot
  visualiser moving with the music where the buttons used to be. The Wheel plays, pauses and skips,
  the way the original did; the light in the title bar tells you which it is.
- **Add an online song to a playlist:** Now Playing ▸ More offers Add to playlist for an online song
  as well as one of your own, putting it in one of your online playlists. Your two libraries'
  playlists stay separate, as they always have.

### Changed
- **No duplicates in a playlist:** adding a song to a playlist that already has it no longer puts it
  in twice — anywhere you can add one, from a song's menu, an album, Add songs, or Now Playing ▸
  More, for your own music and for online songs. Playlists that already have it are marked **Added**
  in the list, so you can see it before you choose.
- **The screen relaxes when the tray hides:** with Now Playing's tray set to hide, the cover, the
  song, the seek bar and the orb now ease down into the space it leaves instead of staying put, and
  draw back up when it returns. On both layouts.
