# Podium 0.1.2

Released 2026-10-12. Tag `v0.1.2`. The full list of changes is in
[CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md); the decisions behind them are D-73 to D-80 in
[decision-log.md](../decision-log.md).

**Podium 0.1.2** adds a sleep timer, a built-in equaliser, crossfade and a new cover slide. It
also fixes the YouTube Music sign-in that could keep loading.

### What's new
- **Sleep timer:** stops the music after 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, at the end of the song or
  at the end of the album. The last 10 seconds fade out before it pauses. In Now Playing ▸ More
  and in Settings.
- **Equaliser:** ten bands from 31 Hz to 16 kHz, thirteen presets or your own curve. Each band
  is set with the Wheel and heard as you turn. In Settings.
- **Crossfade:** songs overlap by 2 to 12 seconds. An album's tracks that run into each other
  stay gapless. In Settings, off until you turn it on.
- **Cover slide:** whenever a new song starts, the playing cover slides off to the left as the
  next comes in from the right, even when both share a cover. Previous runs the other way.
- **Go to album and Go to an artist** in Now Playing ▸ More, with one entry for each artist, for
  local and online songs alike.
- Now Playing says when the sound comes from a video: "Audio from the music video" or "Audio
  from a video".

### Changed
- A song played from search is followed by songs like it, not by the rest of the search results.
- Search lists songs first, then albums. Among albums, full albums come before EPs and singles.

### Fixed
- **Sign-in that kept loading** after the password or 2-step verification. A failed account
  check no longer reloads the page and checks again in a loop: it says what went wrong, with Try
  again and Start over.
- Closing or turning the sign-in screen during the account check no longer leaves the account
  stuck on "Signing in".
- On a slow connection, sign-in now says after 15 seconds that the check can take a while. The
  check isn't cut short.
- Sign-in keeps watching for the session on every page of Google's sign-in, not just for the
  first minute.
- In regions where YouTube Music shows a consent page first, the account check now gets through.
- A session the phone can't store securely is refused with a message instead of crashing the app.
- Go to album and Go to an artist now appear for online songs. A song's album and artist pages
  are looked up when a list leaves them out. A failed lookup is tried again, and what a lookup
  finds is kept.

### Known limitations
- The sign-in and Go to fixes are covered by tests, but not yet confirmed on a phone. The
  sign-in issue on GitHub stays open until they are.
- How closely crossfade lines the two songs up hasn't been measured on a phone.
- With the equaliser on, the sound is no longer bit-for-bit what the file holds. The quality
  label still describes the source.

### Install
1. Download **podium.apk** from the release.
2. Open it on your phone and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. Podium installs over 0.1.1 and keeps your library, finishes and settings.

Needs Android 10 or later. Signed with the official Podium release key (`CN=Podium`). Licensed
under the GNU GPL v3.

### Verify
The SHA-256 of `podium.apk` is published with the GitHub release. `apksigner verify
--print-certs podium.apk` names `CN=Podium`.
