# Podium 0.1.4

Released 2026-10-26. Tag `v0.1.4`. The full list of changes is in
[CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md); the decisions behind them are D-91 to D-100 in
[decision-log.md](../decision-log.md).

**Podium 0.1.4** fixes online songs that wouldn't play, updates itself from GitHub, and adds
custom sources, lossless mid-song and a still background for lyrics.

### What's new
- **Online songs play again:** Podium finds a YouTube Music song's stream the way BitChord does,
  asking as several of YouTube's own apps and checking each stream before it starts, with
  YouTube's TV app as a backup. When a song still can't play, Now Playing says why
  (issue #5).
- **Updates from inside Podium:** Podium checks GitHub for a new version and shows what's new.
  Download and install it right there (Android asks you to confirm), open it on GitHub, or
  ignore it. Settings ▸ Software update has Check now.
- **Custom sources:** add your own stream sources (addons that speak the BitChord addon protocol)
  in Settings ▸ Custom sources. When one has the exact same recording, the song plays from it,
  lossless if that's what it serves.
- **Lossless mid-song:** when your custom sources are slow, a song starts from YouTube Music and
  moves to the lossless copy once it has loaded.
- **A still background for lyrics:** Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Virtual display ▸ Lyrics background
  keeps Classic lyrics on black or on white instead of turning with every line.
- **Switch channel:** Settings ▸ YouTube Music always shows Channel when you're signed in.

### Fixed
- The first online song after opening Podium no longer stalls.
- Custom sources show all their settings, and keep working by themselves when they come back.
- Apple Lossless files play even where the phone's own decoder fails (issue #6).
- Cover Flow opens and closes without the pause.
