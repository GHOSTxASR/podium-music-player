# Podium 0.1.5

Released 2026-11-02. Tag `v0.1.5`. The full list of changes is in
[CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md); the decisions behind them are D-101 to D-108 in
[decision-log.md](../decision-log.md).

**Podium 0.1.5** redesigns Carbon around the album's own light, gives Now Playing a vinyl record
and a play button that is alive, adds playlists for the music on your phone, and rebuilds every
touch the device answers.

### What's new
- **The orb:** Now Playing's play button is a little creature with two eyes. Round while the music
  plays, a settled pebble when you stop it. It dances with the bass, now and then rolls right
  forward over itself in a burst of stars, watches the covers slide by when you change songs, and
  follows the Wheel around the action tray. Leave the music stopped and it sits up, wonders, leans
  in, gets cross, has a small panic for your attention, goes sad, and dozes off.
- **Carbon, redesigned:** the playing album lights Carbon's display — a deep, defocused field of the
  cover's colours — and gives it one accent. Controls are solid, rounded surfaces you can press, the
  seek bar ripples while the music plays, and opening a menu softens everything behind it. The
  hardware is blackened steel, with a spun-metal Wheel whose shine follows the phone as you tilt it.
- **Vinyl:** Now Playing ▸ Vinyl lifts the cover out, slides the record from it and sets it on a
  turntable. The tonearm swings on, a dot visualiser moves with the sound, and ⏮ and ⏭ roll the
  record away for the next one.
- **Playlists of your own music:** Music ▸ Playlists. Make one, name it, fill it from Add songs or
  from any song's menu, and reorder it with the Wheel. Your online playlists stay separate.
- **Haptics, rebuilt:** every touch says what it did. The Wheel clicks like a bearing, ticks faster
  in a spin and thuds at the end of a list; toggles rise and fall, play is a firm click and pause a
  soft fall. Settings ▸ Haptics has five profiles where the phone's motor allows it.
- **A new start-up:** Podium's P appears in a lens of fine lines, spins, unrolls into the PODIUM
  wordmark, and you dive through the O into the menu. Any Wheel press skips it.
- **Let Now Playing clear itself:** Settings ▸ Now Playing's tray slides the row of actions away a
  few seconds after you stop touching anything. Any press of the Wheel brings it back and does
  nothing else, so nothing is skipped or paused by accident.

### Changed
- **One Now Playing for every theme:** the cover flat and large, the controls in one group, the
  other actions in one tray. Only the material changes with the theme.
- **Bolder, smoother, clearer:** text and icons a step bolder, Apple's continuous corners
  everywhere outside the instrument, and one spacing system — 10 dp from the display's edges on
  every side.
- **The title bar:** the back key and title on the left; the time in its own housing on the right,
  with the player, the play state and the battery sharing another. Housings bud and pinch like
  drops of liquid.
- **Livelier colours:** Now Playing's colours are richer and travel all around the display, and a
  new song's colours blend in gradually however quickly you skip.

### Removed
- **Bone:** the warm off-white display has been withdrawn; the display is Glass, Carbon or Custom.
  Custom still draws the same matte instrument on a colour or picture of your own, and the Classic
  lyrics keep their off-white paper. If you were on Bone, Podium opens on Glass.
- **Rotation:** Podium is portrait only. It is a device you hold upright.

### Fixed
- Two playlists with the same name no longer stop Podium in Online ▸ Playlists or Add to playlist.
- Dragging a song in Up Next by its handle puts it where you let go.
- On Glass, Now Playing is tinted by the playing cover again; the device body keeps its neutral light.
